How it works

Clause Guard AI helps you read contracts with confidence, in four steps.

1

Paste or upload

Drop in any agreement as text, a .txt file or a .pdf (up to 60,000 characters). PDF text is extracted in your browser.

2

We check it against your policy

Open “Edit your policy rules” in the review panel to adjust the standards we check — renewal terms, liability caps, late fees and more.

3

Read the alerts

Risks are sorted high, then medium, then passed checks — each with a plain summary and the rule it was checked against.

4

Verify every alert

Click any alert to jump to the exact clause it came from in the original text. Toggle “Plain English” to see a simple explanation under each highlight.
